NetCordon

com.sachinmandawi.netcordon

A standalone, zero-root Android Firewall and Data Traffic Guardian. Powered by Shizuku and native Android NetworkPolicy, NetCordon provides total, granular control over per-app Wi-Fi and Cellular access with zero VPN latency.

🛡️ 3-Tier Firewall

Smart Shield, Total Blackout, or Unrestricted access per package.

⚡ Zero-Root Latency

Direct Linux netpolicy rule injection via Shizuku binder IPC.

📡 Real-Time Radar

Instant background data leak notifications & tracker severance.

🔒 Biometric Lock

Fingerprint, face, and hardware-backed device PIN authentication.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • Applicable to NetCordon & all distributed utilities.

1. Zero Data Harvesting

NetCordon is architected from the ground up to respect your digital sovereignty. We do not collect, intercept, inspect, store, or sell any network traffic, visited URLs, DNS queries, or app activity transmitted through your device.

2. Local On-Device Execution

All firewall rules, data quotas, hourly schedules, and telemetry statistics are stored and processed strictly within your device's encrypted local storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences and internal application database). No telemetry or tracking data leaves your device.

3. Advertising & Monetization

NetCordon integrates the official Google AdMob SDK to support development. AdMob may utilize standard mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and approximate coarse location to serve compliant, non-intrusive advertisements in adherence with Google Play and Amazon Appstore developer policies.

4. Permissions Usage

  • Network State & Internet: Used solely to monitor connectivity status and render served ads.
  • Package Usage Stats: Queried locally to measure per-app network consumption and alert you of silent background data drain.
  • Biometric: Facilitates on-device app locking via Android BiometricPrompt; biometric data never leaves hardware secure enclaves.

5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions, bug reports, or suggestions regarding NetCordon or this privacy policy, please reach out directly at sachinmandawi@gmail.com.
